A fragment's first line is its header. A mismatch voids the whole fragment. Once the header is good, a duplicate header row appearing again later, or any other malformed data row - wrong column count, or non-numeric where a number is expected - is skipped on its own; it does not void the fragment, and rows after it are still eligible for recovery. Values are exact and whitespace-trimmed. Recovered rows follow the artifact lifecycle: STORE, HIT, MISS, EVICT, REFRESH. A HIT, EVICT, or REFRESH repeats the last STORE's content-hash and size. REFRESH keeps a stored or evicted artifact in the same state. Timestamps never decrease, though repeats are valid. Recovery starts at genesis and goes as far as any linked, hash-valid, lifecycle-valid row allows. Ties go to the alphabetically first filename.
